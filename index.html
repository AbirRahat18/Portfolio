I’m Abir Ahmed Rahat, a frontend developer in Dhaka. I build clear, responsive websites and useful React interfaces, bringing together careful design and practical development.

I studied Law at the National University of Bangladesh, earning a Bachelor of Arts, and completed Programming Hero’s Frontend Web Developer program in 2025. My hands-on work includes responsive Justice Law, Book Library, and Architecture portfolio projects. I build with HTML, CSS, Tailwind, JavaScript, React, React Router, REST APIs, and GitHub, focusing on accessible, easy-to-use experiences across screen sizes.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What kind of projects do you take on?

I work with early-stage teams, independent founders, and creative studios on websites, product interfaces, and frontend builds. If you care about the details, we’ll probably get along.

What does working together look like?

We start with a short conversation to understand the people, goals, and scope. From there I share a clear plan, regular progress updates, and a collaborative review before handoff.

Can you work with an existing design?

Absolutely. I can partner with your designer or team to bring an existing Figma file to life, refine responsive behavior, and make sure the implementation feels as good as it looks.

Are you available for freelance work?

I take on a small number of projects at a time. Share a little about your idea and timeline, and I’ll get back to you with availability and next steps.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope and complexity. Once I understand your goals, I’ll share a realistic timeline and keep you updated at each step.

Can you help after the website launches?

Yes. I can help with follow-up fixes and improvements after launch, with ongoing support arranged around your needs.
